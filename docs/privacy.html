2. What this policy covers

This policy describes which personal data is processed when you visit foresprint.ing, and what the Foresprint desktop application does with data (section 7). The website is a static information page. It does not use cookies, analytics, tracking tools, contact forms, user accounts, or content loaded from third-party servers; fonts and icons are served from the site itself.

3. Hosting

The website is served by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open a page, your browser connects to GitHub’s servers, which process technical connection data such as your IP address, the date and time of the request, the requested page, and browser and operating system details. GitHub acts on our behalf to deliver the site and may process this data on its own servers, including servers in the USA. Where data is transferred outside the European Union, this relies on the safeguards that the provider uses under Chapter V GDPR.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: we have a legitimate interest in delivering this website securely and reliably.

4. Domain name resolution (DNS)

The domain of this website is resolved through the DNS service of Cloudflare. Cloudflare is used for name resolution only (“DNS only”); the traffic of the website does not run through Cloudflare. When the domain name is resolved, technically necessary connection data (e.g., the IP address of the requesting party) may be processed by the name servers. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in the reliable availability of the website).

5. Server log data

Each request to the website is logged by the hosting provider. Such logs typically contain the IP address, the time of the request, the requested address, the referring page, and the browser and operating system in use. We do not have access to these logs and do not combine this data with other sources. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: logging is needed to run and protect the service.

6. Contacting us

If you write to us by e-mail, we process your message and the details you give (such as your name and e-mail address) in order to answer you. We do not pass this on to others without your consent. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your message relates to a contract or pre-contractual steps, and otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our interest in handling enquiries). We delete your message once your matter is resolved, unless a statutory retention duty applies.

7. The application

This section concerns the Foresprint desktop application, not a visit to this website. The application stores its data locally and in the Git repository that your team sets up for it, and does not send it to the operator of this website. It connects only to the Jira and Git servers that you set up yourself.

In addition, the application checks once a day whether a new version is available. For this it retrieves the list of releases from GitHub (see the section “Hosting”); GitHub processes technically necessary connection data in the process, in particular your IP address. Nothing is installed automatically. The check can be switched off with foresprint update --auto off. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in informing users about new versions).

8. Recipients and storage period

Your data is passed on only to the service providers named above, to the extent they need it to deliver the service, or where the law requires or permits disclosure. We keep personal data only as long as the purpose requires it or a statutory retention duty applies; server logs are kept by the hosting provider under its own retention rules.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • obtain confirmation of whether we process personal data about you, and access to that data (Art. 15);
  • have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 16);
  • have your data erased where the legal conditions are met (Art. 17);
  • have processing restricted, for example while the accuracy of data is being checked or while an objection is being assessed (Art. 18);
  • receive data you provided in a common, machine-readable format, where processing is based on consent or a contract (Art. 20);
  • withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future, without affecting earlier processing (Art. 7(3));
  • complain to a supervisory authority, in particular in the member state of your habitual residence, your place of work, or the place of the alleged infringement (Art. 77).

To exercise these rights, write to the e-mail address in section 1.

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR)

Where we process your data on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you may object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation. We will then stop processing unless we can show compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing serves to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. We do not use personal data for direct marketing or profiling.

10. Security

The website is delivered over an encrypted HTTPS connection, so that the data exchanged between your browser and the server cannot be read by third parties on the way. You can recognise this by “https://” and the lock symbol in your browser. Please note that e-mail in general cannot be protected completely against access by third parties.

11. Changes

We may update this policy when the website or the application changes or when legal requirements change. The current version is the one published on this page.
